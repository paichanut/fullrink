Mobile VAR

by FullRink · developed by AfterShoot

Video review from your pocket

Mobile VAR brings video review to the bench. Freeze the play, step through it frame by frame and compare angles so officials and coaches can check a goal or a close call without leaving the rink. It is in development; the page will be updated when a build is ready to try.

What it does

  • Review a clip on the phone right after the play
  • Freeze and single-frame stepping
  • Side-by-side angles from more than one camera
  • Built to work with clips from DejaVu Cam and Live By
