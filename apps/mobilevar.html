mobileVAR

Video review from your pocket

mobileVAR brings video review to the bench. Freeze the play, step through it frame by frame and compare angles so officials and coaches can check a goal or a close call without leaving the rink. It is in development; the page will be updated when a build is ready to try.

Ask about early access

What it does

  • Review a clip on the phone right after the play
  • Freeze and single-frame stepping
  • Side-by-side angles from more than one camera
  • Built to work with clips from dejavucam and liveby
