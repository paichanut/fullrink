Live By

by FullRink · developed by AfterShoot

Live-streaming camera with overlays

Live By turns an Android phone into a live broadcast camera for rink sports. Its first overlay is an ice-hockey scoreboard you control from another phone over the internet, and you can layer images, video, text, music and replays on top of the stream. Every feature is free.

Join the test on Google Play Original site: live.aftershoot.co.th

In testing on Google Play (package th.co.aftershoot.liveby).

The scoreboard control page for each event is at the link the app generates, not on this page.

What it does

  • Stream live from the phone camera to your audience
  • Hockey scoreboard overlay: period, clock, score, shots on goal and penalties
  • Control the scoreboard from a second phone, anywhere with internet
  • Overlays for images, looping video, text, web pages and music
  • Instant replay of the last 10, 20 or 30 seconds, in slow motion
  • Stream to YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, your own RTMP/RTSP, OBS and local recording at once
  • Free, with every feature included: no subscription, no watermark

What it does, in detail

Scoreboard in the picture

Full hockey board: clock, period, score, shots on goal, penalties, team logos. Resize and reposition it, or relabel it for other sports.

Score control from another device

Open the control page on any phone or laptop, on the rink Wi-Fi or over the internet. The scorekeeper never touches the camera.

Clock synced to the real board

Point the camera at the rink's scoreboard and the app reads the time, so the on-screen clock matches the arena.

Instant replay

Rewind the last 10, 20 or 30 seconds, play at 0.5x, with a REPLAY badge so viewers know.

Images, video, text, web pages

Sponsor logos, looping video, on-screen text, web pages. Drag to move, pinch to resize, crop as needed.

Music on the stream

Play music from the phone into the stream. Viewers hear it, the phone speaker stays silent, volume is adjustable.

Stream everywhere at once

YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, your own RTMP/RTSP, OBS, and a local recording, all at the same time.

Clean feed mode

One button shrinks the picture into a corner and moves every control outside the frame, so you see exactly what viewers see.

Every feature is free — No monthly subscription, no watermark, no upgrade screen, unlike live apps that lock images and video behind a paywall.

Getting started

  1. Join the test — Open the Play Store testing link with the Google account you registered, then accept.
  2. Install from the Play Store — After joining, install from the same page.
  3. Pick destinations — Tick YouTube, Facebook or local recording, then press START.
  4. Send the link to the scorekeeper — The badge at the bottom right is the control page address; tap to copy it. You can also set up Telegram in Settings to send the link automatically.
