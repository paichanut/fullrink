liveby

Live-streaming camera with overlays

liveby turns an Android phone into a live broadcast camera for rink sports. Its first overlay is an ice-hockey scoreboard you control from another phone over the internet, and you can layer images, video, text, music and replays on top of the stream. Every feature is free.

Open the liveby website Join the test on Google Play

In testing on Google Play (package th.co.aftershoot.liveby).

What it does

  • Stream live from the phone camera to your audience
  • Hockey scoreboard overlay: period, clock, score and penalties
  • Control the scoreboard from a second phone, anywhere with internet
  • Overlays for images, video clips, text and music
  • Replays on the live stream
  • Free, with every feature included
