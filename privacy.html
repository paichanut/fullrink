Privacy Policy

Effective 7 November 2026. Applies to fullrink.com, the FullRink account service at auth.fullrink.com, and the FullRink apps: Live By, DejaVu Cam, Mobile VAR, Analytic Cam, Live By Scoreboard and GameMap.

Who we are

FullRink is the app brand of AfterShoot, Thailand. Contact for anything in this policy: hello@fullrink.com.

Cookies

The account service sets only the cookies it needs to keep you signed in (a session cookie and a sign-in state cookie). fullrink.com itself stores one setting in your browser: your choice of dark or light mode. Nothing is used for advertising or cross-site tracking.

Where data is stored and who can see it

  • Account data is stored on a server operated by us in Oracle Cloud, Singapore region, behind encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • The website is served by Vercel. Google processes your sign-in when you choose "Sign in with Google", under Google's own privacy policy.
  • We do not sell personal data and we do not share it with advertisers. We share it only with the service providers above, as needed to run the service, or when the law requires it.
  • Rink staff who operate an app see the game data of their own rink, not your account credentials.

How long we keep it

Your account exists until you delete it or ask us to. Sign-in logs are kept for up to 90 days. Game and video data follow the retention set by the rink that operates the app. Encrypted backups are kept for 14 days.

Your rights

You can see and edit your account details at auth.fullrink.com, unlink Google there, and sign out of every device. To delete your account or to get a copy of your data, email hello@fullrink.com from the address on the account and we will do it within 30 days. This policy follows Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA); if you are in another country, the rights of your local law apply too.

Children

FullRink accounts are for rink staff, coaches, players and parents. Children under 13 should not create their own account; a parent or coach can manage the data on their behalf.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will put a notice on fullrink.com and in the sign-in page before the change takes effect. The date at the top shows the current version.
