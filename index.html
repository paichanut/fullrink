The apps

One app for every job on the ice

Each app does one thing well. Use one, or run them together.

liveby

Live-stream the game from an Android phone with overlays on top: a hockey scoreboard you control from another phone, plus images, video, text, music and replays. Free.

Open liveby →

dejavucam

Retroactive recording. dejavucam is always recording to memory, so one tap saves the last 5 to 30 seconds: the goal, the save, the hit. Replay in slow motion and share.

Open dejavucam →

mobileVAR

Video review from your pocket. Freeze, step frame by frame and compare angles so officials and coaches can check a goal or a close call from the bench. In development.

Open mobileVAR →

analytic cam

Skating technique, measured. Record a clip, even at 240 fps, and analytic cam tracks your pose on the phone: lean, knee angles and balance on the replay, then a scored report.

Open analytic cam →

liveby scoreboard

A live hockey scoreboard in any browser. Period, clock, score and penalties, controlled remotely, shown on any screen or overlaid on your liveby stream.

Open liveby scoreboard →
