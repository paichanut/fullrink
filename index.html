For rink owners

What the rink gets, not what the app does

01

Teams and leagues pick your rink

Games with a live stream, an on-screen scoreboard and replays are the games teams want to play and leagues want to host. You stop competing on hourly rate alone.

02

New sponsor revenue

Sponsor logos sit on the stream every game, every minute. Sell them as season packages. You place them yourself, from the phone.

03

More viewers, no more seats

Parents watch from work, away teams watch from another province. Stream to the rink's YouTube and Facebook at the same time.

04

One person, no equipment

One phone and a tripod in a corner of the rink. The scorekeeper taps on their own phone from the bench. No switcher, no camera cables, no monthly fee.

All 6 apps

Built one at a time, made to work together

01 · LIVE BY

A live camera with the scoreboard in the picture

Streams to YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and RTMP at once. The hockey scoreboard is overlaid on the video and controlled by the scorekeeper from their own phone over the internet. Point the camera at the rink's real board to sync the clock.

Live By detailsTesting on Google Play
02 · DEJAVU CAM

Tap after it happens, get the clip instantly

The app records to memory all the time. When there's a goal, a save or a hit, one tap saves the last 5 to 30 seconds. Slow it down, step frame by frame, share it to the team chat.

DejaVu Cam detailsTesting on Google Play
03 · MOBILE VAR

Settle close calls from the bench

Step through frame by frame, compare camera angles, and check a goal or a disputed play on the phone without waiting for the replay.

Mobile VAR detailsIn development
In developmentOpening for testing soon. Ask for early access on the app page.
04 · ANALYTIC CAM

Skating technique, analysed on the phone

Record or import a clip. The app tracks the skeleton on every frame, shows lean, left and right knee angles and balance, then scores the technique. 240 fps slow motion on supported phones.

Analytic Cam detailsTesting on Google Play
05 · LIVE BY SCOREBOARD

A scoreboard in the browser, on any screen

Period, clock, score and penalties, updated live. Put it on the rink display, share the link with viewers, or overlay it on a Live By stream.

06 · GAMEMAP

See the game from above

Open a game clip in the browser and mark each player's position on a top-down rink as the video plays. Get movement data for every player. Works with clips from Live By or DejaVu Cam.

Match day

The whole set in one game

Before the gameSet up the camera, open the board

  • Mount the phone running Live By where it sees the whole rink; add both team logos
  • Open Live By Scoreboard on a screen or laptop by the bench
  • Send the score-control link to the scorekeeper, via Telegram straight from the app

During the gameStream, keep score, catch the moment

  • Live By streams everywhere with the scoreboard on screen
  • The scorekeeper taps on their own phone and never touches the camera
  • A second phone runs DejaVu Cam: when there's a goal, one tap and the clip is ready
  • A disputed play goes into Mobile VAR, frame by frame

After the gameReview form, share clips

  • Put each skater's clip into Analytic Cam for knee angles, balance and a score
  • Share DejaVu Cam replays to the team chat
  • The full game recording is already on the phone
