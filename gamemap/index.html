Camera calibration

One camera never sees the whole rink, so calibration is a set of moments in the clip: pause, click a landmark on the video, then the same spot on the map, at least 4 pairs. When the camera pans and the rink lines drawn over the video drift, add another set; the map blends between sets.

Teams

After AI processing these follow the detected jerseys automatically; change them any time

Tag positions by hand

Pause the clip, choose a player and click the rink to record where they are at that moment, or, once the camera is calibrated, turn on “Tag from video” and click the player’s feet in the clip. Movement between recorded points is filled in.
